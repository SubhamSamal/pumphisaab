## What this day tells us about the app (see questions)
1. Meters, testing and money are recorded **once for the whole day**, not per shift.
2. The day's money check is a **running cash book** (opening cash + in − out = cash in hand), not "Should have vs Received" per shift.
3. **Payments received** are customers settling credit dues (by bank transfer or XtraPower), which the PRD had put out of scope.
4. Most credit slips are **round rupee fills** (₹14,000), with litres worked out from the price; a few are litre fills (420 L, 1,600 L).
5. Credit customers can take **cash** (S.V.T. ₹11,000), recorded both on their credit list and as an expense.
6. The pump uses **Paytm**, not PhonePe; XtraPower and card swipes have **adjustments** and **not-yet-settled** amounts.
7. Nozzles 1 and 2 sold nothing on both fuels.
